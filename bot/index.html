If you've seen Unitable/0.1 or UnitableBot/0.1 in your server logs, it's us. Unitable is a student-made timetable app, currently in testing at ANU.

Unitable/0.1: students' own calendar feeds

When a student connects their timetable (e.g. MyTimetable) or Canvas calendar, Unitable fetches that student's private calendar feed on their behalf, so their app stays up to date. It only fetches feeds students have connected themselves, and never signs in for them.

  • Each feed is checked at most once an hour, at the start of semester; every 3 hours during teaching and exams; every 12 hours in breaks.
  • A student opening the app can trigger an earlier check, at most once every 30 minutes.
  • We send If-None-Match / If-Modified-Since, so unchanged feeds cost a 304.
  • Failing feeds back off, and links that stop working are no longer fetched.

UnitableBot/0.1: public pages

Unitable reads a few public uni pages, such as room bookings and key dates, and caches them centrally, so each student's app doesn't fetch them separately. These are refreshed no faster than the pages change, at most every few hours.
