Privacy policy

Unitable · last updated 19 November 2026

Unitable shows your uni timetable, due dates, free rooms and friends' free time in one app. This page explains what it stores, why, who helps run it, and how to delete everything. Unitable is run by an individual student developer in Australia and follows the Australian Privacy Principles.

The short version

  • We only store what the app needs to work. No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell data.
  • We never see or store your uni password. You sign in to MyTimetable and Canvas on your uni's own pages.
  • Your private calendar links are encrypted and never shown again, even to you.
  • Friends only see what you choose to share with each of them.
  • Settings → Delete my account removes everything, straight away.

What we store

WhatWhy
Your uni email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code, and to know which uni you're at.
Your name, if you add one, and your friend codeSo friends can find and recognise you.
The private calendar links for your timetable (e.g. MyTimetable) and CanvasTo keep your classes and due dates up to date. Stored encrypted.
Your classes, due dates and recent changes to themTo show your timetable and tell you when something changes.
Classes you've removed or marked as freeSo your timetable and free time stay as you set them.
Your friends, friend requests and what you share with eachTo show free time together, and to share only what you allow.
Whether you've turned on class matchingOff by default. When on, friends can see which of your classes you share.
A notification token for your device, and the notifications sent to youTo send alerts like class changes. You can turn these off.
A private link for calendar apps, if you create oneSo a calendar app can show your timetable. Only a scrambled form of the link is stored.

The app also keeps a copy of your timetable on your phone so it works offline. If you sign in to MyTimetable or Canvas inside Unitable, your phone remembers that university sign-in so you don't have to sign in again; only Unitable on your phone can use it, and we never see your password. Both are removed when you sign out of Unitable.

Unitable also shows public uni information, like room bookings and key dates, taken from your uni's public websites. That isn't about you.

Who helps run Unitable

  • Supabase hosts the database and servers, in Sydney, Australia.
  • Expo builds the app and passes notifications to Apple.
  • Apple delivers notifications to your iPhone and runs TestFlight and the App Store.
  • Resend sends the sign-in code emails.

They only get what they need to do that job. We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Calendar links are encrypted with keys kept separately from the database. Each person can only read their own data, and friends only see what you've chosen to share with them.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Disconnecting your timetable or Canvas deletes its link and the classes or due dates from it. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account and data) removes everything about you at once. Our hosting provider's backups may hold a copy for up to 7 days before it's gone for good.

Your choices

  • Change what each friend sees, or remove them, at any time.
  • Turn class matching and notifications on or off in Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, using the contact below.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@unitable.net. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
