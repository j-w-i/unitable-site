    Questions

    Which universities does it work with?

    ANU for now. Unitable is built so other universities can be added, so tell us if you'd like yours next.

    Is it made by ANU?

    No. Unitable is an independent app made by an ANU student. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Australian National University.

    How does it get my timetable?

    MyTimetable and Canvas both give every student a private calendar link. You sign in once inside Unitable, it finds those links, and it checks them for changes from then on.

    How do I join the beta?

    Unitable is on TestFlight, Apple's app for trying apps before they're on the App Store. Tap "Get the beta on TestFlight" on your iPhone. If you don't have TestFlight yet, the App Store will offer it first; then install Unitable from it.

    Is there an Android version?

    It's coming. The beta is iPhone first.

    Can my friends see my timetable?

    Only if you let them. When you add a friend you choose what they see: nothing, just when you're busy, or your whole timetable, and you can change it any time. Joining a group shares when you're free and busy with the people in it.

    What does it cost?

    Unitable is free. The one paid extra is an optional calendar link, $2.99 a year, if you also want your classes in your phone's calendar app.
